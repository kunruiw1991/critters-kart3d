# 🏎️🌈🏆 Critter Kart 3D: Rainbow Grand Prix! (Three.js 3D WebGL Engine)

🎮 **Play Live (GitHub Pages):** **[https://kunruiw1991.github.io/critters-kart3d/](https://kunruiw1991.github.io/critters-kart3d/)**

A **Full-Screen 3D WebGL (`Three.js`) Kart Racing & Sky Glider Adventure** starring all **8 Smiling Critters** (**DogDay**, **CatNap**, **CraftyCorn**, **Hoppy Hopscotch**, **PickyPiggy**, **Bubba Bubbaphant**, **Bobby BearHug**, and **KickinChicken**)!

---

## ✨ 3D Engine & Gameplay Highlights

- **Real-Time Three.js 3D WebGL Engine (`100vw × 100vh`)**:
  - Sculpted 3D Smiling Critter Karts with aerodynamic chassis, front nose cones, side pods, rear spoiler wings, 4 spinning 3D racing wheels, custom 3D driver heads & species-specific 3D ears/horns, and deployable **3D Rainbow Glider Wings**!
  - Dynamic 3D chase camera with `🍄` Turbo speed-rush FOV widening and a **360° Orbiting 3D Podium Camera** at the finish line!
- **100% Zero-Text Visual Racing Cockpit**:
  - Designed for young players with **zero text** anywhere — guided by a live **3D Race Progress Gauge** (`🏎️ ─── ⭐ ─── ⭐ ─── ⭐ ─── 🏁`), a vertical 8-Critter 3D World & Kart Switcher on the left, and big tactile 3D arcade pedals along the bottom.
- **Intuitive Steering, Gliding & Mario-Kart 3D Power-Ups**:
  - `⬅️` / `➡️` **5-Lane 3D Drift Steering**: Tap the left/right pedals, tap/swipe directly on the 3D highway, or use `⬅️`/`➡️` arrow keys to drift across 5 lanes!
  - `🪽` **3D Rainbow Glider Wings**: Leap into the sky and unfold 3D rainbow wings to soar over obstacles and collect high-altitude Sky Stars!
  - `🍄` **Golden Mushroom Turbo**: Ignites twin 3D exhaust flames, boosts top speed, and smashes straight through 3D obstacle crates!
  - `🐢` **Homing Rainbow Shell**: Fires a spinning 3D green-and-white shell down the highway that bowls over obstacles and spins out rival karts!
  - `🧲` **Mega Star Magnet**: Activates a spinning 3D purple aura ring that pulls nearby 3D Gold Stars across all 5 lanes straight into your kart!
- **8 Distinct 3D Biome Grand Prix Cups**:
  1. ☀️ **DogDay's Sunny Meadow Speedway**
  2. 🌙 **CatNap's Starlight Twilight Highway**
  3. 🎨 **CraftyCorn's Pastel Rainbow Circuit**
  4. ⚡ **Hoppy's Emerald Forest Dash**
  5. 🍎 **PickyPiggy's Pink Berry Boulevard**
  6. ❄️ **Bubba's Frosty Glacier Expressway**
  7. 💖 **Bobby's Ruby Heart Canyon**
  8. 🔥 **KickinChicken's Golden Sunset Grand Prix**
